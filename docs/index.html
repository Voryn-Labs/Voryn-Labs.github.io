The Flagship Trio

Each application is built from first principles for a singular purpose — engineered with transparent receipts, zero data harvesting, and complete user agency.

iPhone · iOS 26+ Released · v0.4

Cloudified

Bit-for-bit uncompressed media backup to two destinations.

Back up original photos and 4K videos directly to Google Photos and private Telegram channels at the same time. Cloudified executes completely on your iPhone with zero intermediate servers, managing independent queues, local SQLite receipts, and verified recovery before retrying.

  • Dual Concurrent Queues — Google Photos and Telegram move independently without blocking.
  • Bit-for-Bit Originals — Unaltered files and Live Photos preserved with full fidelity.
  • Offline SQLite Receipts — Confirmed content is recorded locally; zero blind duplicate resends.
  • 100% On-Device — No middleman server, no telemetry SDK, zero credential harvesting.

Musyn

Music, in your rhythm. Dual sources, synced lyrics, and offline cache.

One distraction-free home for your favorite audio. Musyn unifies streaming from YouTube Music and JioSaavn with dynamic time-synced lyrics that scroll with the music, full queue management, and one-tap offline downloads for listening anywhere without internet.

  • Unified Dual Catalogs — YouTube Music and JioSaavn accessible in one unified player.
  • Time-Synced Lyrics — Dynamic lyrics that follow along in rhythm with the music.
  • Local Offline Downloads — Save tracks directly to your device storage for offline playback.
  • Queue You Control — Zero algorithmic injection, zero dark patterns, no subscriptions.

Android TV

VividOrbit

A lightweight, fully offline live-TV and lineup manager for Android TV and Google TV. Renumber your channels the way you actually watch them, and manage the whole lineup from your phone over a QR code — nothing leaves your living room.

  • Custom channel numbering — reorder to 1..N, with atomic swaps so numbers never collide.
  • Phone as remote — a local web UI over QR code; edit, reorder and tune from your phone.
  • Now / Next EPG — live programme progress that collapses cleanly when a source has no data.
  • 100% offline — zero cloud dependency, zero accounts, zero telemetry.

The Studio Creed

Why Voryn Labs

Software should serve you quietly. We build for the long haul with three uncompromised engineering tenets.

  1. 01

    Calm by design

    No fail states, no streak anxiety, no manipulative push notifications. Software you can set down and return to without penalty — every tool stays out of your way.

  2. 02

    Fair by default

    Deterministic rules, zero telemetry, and offline wherever possible. What you create and store remains yours alone; no arbitrary cloud gatekeeping.

  3. 03

    Zero dark patterns

    No disguised subscriptions, no third-party trackers, no ad networks harvesting your identity. Your attention and your data are guests, never the product.
