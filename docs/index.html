Android TV

VividOrbit

A lightweight, fully offline live-TV and lineup manager for Android TV and Google TV. Renumber your channels the way you actually watch them, and manage the whole lineup from your phone over a QR code — nothing leaves your living room.

  • Custom channel numbering — reorder to 1..N, with atomic swaps so numbers never collide.
  • Phone as remote — a local web UI over QR code; edit, reorder and tune from your phone.
  • Now / Next EPG — live programme progress that collapses cleanly when a source has no data.
  • 100% offline — zero cloud dependency, zero accounts, zero telemetry.

The creed

Why Voryn Labs

  1. 01

    Calm by design

    No fail states, no punishing timers, no nagging. Software you can set down and pick back up without penalty — every game is completeable, every app stays out of your way.

  2. 02

    Fair by default

    No-guess logic in every puzzle. Deterministic rules, no pay-to-win, no artificial scarcity — and where an app can work offline, it works offline. What you earn is earned; what you own is yours.

  3. 03

    No dark patterns

    No fake urgency, no manipulative pop-ups, no disguised subscriptions. One-time Remove Ads purchases, never a subscription trap. Your attention is a guest, not the product.

The studio

About Voryn Labs

Voryn Labs is an independent studio making quiet, considered software for Android. We started with games — the Lumen Series, then the India Collection — and we build across categories now: the Living Room collection opens with VividOrbit for Android TV, and more will follow. One design creed throughout, whatever the category: calm, fair, honest. We build for the long haul, and we respect your time, your data, and your attention.
