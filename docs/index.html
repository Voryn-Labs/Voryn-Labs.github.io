The creed

Why Voryn Labs

  1. 01

    Calm by design

    No fail states. No punishing timers. Games you can set down and pick back up without penalty — every title is completeable.

  2. 02

    Fair by default

    No-guess logic in every puzzle. Deterministic rules, no pay-to-win, no artificial scarcity. What you earn is earned.

  3. 03

    No dark patterns

    No fake urgency, no manipulative pop-ups, no disguised subscriptions. One-time Remove Ads purchases, never a subscription trap. Your attention is a guest, not the product.

The studio

About Voryn Labs

Voryn Labs is an independent studio making quiet, considered puzzle games for Android. Fourteen titles, one design creed: calm, fair, honest. We build for the long haul — small games that respect your time, your data, and your attention.
