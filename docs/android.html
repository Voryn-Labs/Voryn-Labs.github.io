Sovereignty & Zero Telemetry

Handheld Architecture vs. Living Room Television

Why Voryn Labs maintains a strict separation between Android TV and Android Handheld devices.

VividOrbit: Living Room Android TV

VividOrbit is an actively released broadcast guide and channel lineup manager designed specifically for ten-foot living room navigation with D-pad remotes and local QR pairing. It targets Android TV and Google TV devices exclusively.

Handheld Android: In Active R&D

Smartphones have distinct threat models, battery constraints, and background scheduling systems. We do not repackage TV interfaces or ship half-baked mobile shells. Our handheld tools will launch only when they meet our strict offline sovereignty standards.

Four Sovereign Pillars

Every mobile line of code under development in our private labs adheres to four non-negotiable standards.

1. Zero Google Play Services Dependency

Engineered to execute flawlessly on de-Googled operating systems including GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, LineageOS, and vanilla AOSP. Zero reliance on proprietary Google Play APIs or SafetyNet beacons.

2. F-Droid & Signed APK Distribution

Direct cryptographic signed APK releases alongside upcoming official F-Droid repository distribution with automated reproducible builds. You maintain total installation sovereignty.

3. 100% Offline-First Ledgers

Deterministic local data models, atomic receipt ledgers, and zero tracking SDKs harvesting device fingerprints. What runs on your phone stays exclusively on your phone.

4. Multi-Platform Ecosystem Companions

Handheld companion tooling designed to interface seamlessly with our existing Musyn audio caching and Cloudified bit-for-bit uncompressed backup architectures.
