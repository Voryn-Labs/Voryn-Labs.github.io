Privacy Policy — Cloudified

Cloudified is an uncompressed photo backup accelerator for iOS. It connects your native iOS Photos library directly to your personal Google Photos and Telegram destinations with cryptographic local ledgers.

Effective: October 16, 2026 · Bundle: com.vorynlabs.cloudified
Direct Destination Routing

Media streams directly to your personal Google account or Telegram storage channel. Zero intermediary Voryn servers.

Keychain Stored Credentials

Tokens and master credentials stay strictly inside hardware-backed iOS Keychain. Never uploaded to diagnostics.

Local Cryptographic Receipts

Every photo backup is indexed locally with SHA-256 hashes to prevent duplicates without remote telemetry indexing.

1. Destination Routing & Direct Data Streams

Your media uploads directly to the personal Google account or Telegram channel you authenticate. Cloudified stages files locally, computes bit-exact SHA-256 checksums, and records local receipts in an encrypted SQLite database on your iPhone.

At no point does your photo media pass through any Voryn Labs cloud server or proxy. All network transfers occur over encrypted TLS directly between your iOS device and Google or Telegram APIs.

2. Credentials & Keychain Security

Credentials, auth tokens, and local database keys are stored strictly in the hardware-backed iOS Keychain on your physical device. We provide no remote authentication proxy; you interact with the third-party provider directly.

3. Ephemeral Redacted Diagnostics

If you choose to submit a debug report, the app generates a sanitized JSON preview stripping authentication tokens, personal names, account emails, and image filenames. Reports expire after seven days and must be explicitly reviewed by you prior to submission.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising SDKs

Cloudified contains zero commercial advertising networks, zero tracking cookies, and zero behavioral analytics libraries.

5. Direct Studio Correspondence

Inquiries regarding Cloudified’s security architecture or cryptographic integrity: appsvorynlabs@gmail.com.
