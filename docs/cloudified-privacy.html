Your files and accounts

Updated 16 October 2026. This page describes Cloudified; the studio’s Android games and VividOrbit have separate policies.

Where media goes

Original media uploads directly to the Google account or Telegram channel you link. The app stages files and keeps receipts, checksums and recovery records locally. Telegram channel archives are cloud documents, not end-to-end encrypted Secret Chats. People with access to that destination may access the media and metadata.

Credentials stay with your installation

Google’s unofficial Google sign-in flow stores an Android master credential and authentication data in the iOS Keychain. That credential has broader scope than a Photos-only token. Cloudified cannot guarantee unlimited quota, continued service availability or isolation from Google’s account and device security systems. Telegram credentials and the local database encryption key use Keychain/native storage. No developer credential server or bundled signing certificate is provided.

Installation and LiveContainer

Changing signing team or bundle ID can affect access to local data and credentials. The public 0.1 identity does not inherit earlier testing data. In LiveContainer, the host controls the guest environment and credential isolation. Use a host you trust; Cloudified cannot provide a separate iOS security boundary inside it.

Optional public diagnostics

When you choose report upload, a redacted diagnostic report goes to dpaste.com and returns a public link. Reports are requested to expire after seven days. They exclude credentials, media, account names and private paths by design, but include timestamps, version/build, counts, device conditions and pseudonymous correlation identifiers. Anyone with the link can read it; copies in issues may last longer. Review before sharing. GitHub opens a draft; uploading logs does not submit an issue automatically.

No advertising or analytics SDK

Cloudified does not include an advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Linked cloud services, GitHub Pages, GitHub, Google Fonts on this site, and diagnostic hosting have their own policies and may receive network requests when used.

Control and contact

You can disable either destination or delink it in Settings. Removing the app removes its local container; Keychain behavior depends on iOS and the signing/host environment. Cloudified does not delete your remote archive when you delink. Questions: appsvorynlabs@gmail.com.
