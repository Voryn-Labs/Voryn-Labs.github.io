Privacy Policy — VividOrbit

VividOrbit is an open-source, privacy-first Live TV channel management and Electronic Program Guide (EPG) application for Android TV and Google TV. Your television habits, channel lineups, and viewing choices belong solely to you.

Effective: August 20, 2026 · Package: com.vorynlabs.vividorbit
Zero Personal Data

No account sign-ups, no names, no email addresses, no phone numbers, and zero device fingerprinting.

Zero Analytics or Ads

Contains zero analytics SDKs, zero third-party crash loggers, zero advertising banners, and no commercial beacons.

100% Local Storage

Channel lists, preferences, and custom categories remain strictly inside your TV's encrypted local app container.

1. Overview & Studio Creed

Voryn Labs engineers deterministic, calm, privacy-first software. We believe your living room entertainment should never be subject to surveillance capitalism, behavioral tracking, or clandestine telemetry beacons.

VividOrbit operates entirely offline for its core channel management workflows. We do not operate user databases or telemetry ingest endpoints.

2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Tracking

VividOrbit does not collect, record, transmit, or monetize any user data.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, accounts, IP records, or unique hardware identifiers.
  • No Telemetry or Crash Trackers: VividOrbit does not include Google Analytics for Firebase, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Sentry, or any background telemetry collectors.
  • No External Server Communication: Your favorite channels, custom numbering, and hidden status lists never leave your TV device.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

VividOrbit requests only the minimal local Android permissions required to interact with your TV's hardware tuner:

  • READ_TV_LISTINGS & READ_EPG_DATA: Reads installed TV tuner channel lists and local Electronic Program Guide (EPG) metadata provided directly by your television tuner hardware.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely for the optional Phone Setup feature. When activated, the app runs a private, temporary HTTP server on your local Wi-Fi network (LAN) so you can sort channels comfortably from your phone. No data is ever sent to the public Internet.

4. Local Phone Setup Server Security

When you explicitly select the "Phone Setup" option, a lightweight HTTP server is launched temporarily on your TV hardware. This connection is:

  • Strictly contained within your private home local area network (LAN).
  • Protected by a high-entropy, ephemeral session token displayed as a QR code on your TV screen.
  • Immediately terminated when you close the Phone Setup screen or navigate away from the application.

5. Open Source Auditability

VividOrbit is 100% open source under the GPL-3.0 license. The application relies on open components from the Android Open Source Project (AOSP), the Mochitif Leanback Engine, and the ZXing QR engine. The complete codebase is publicly auditable on GitHub.

6. Children's Privacy

VividOrbit is a neutral channel utility not directed to children under 13, and it collects zero personal data from any person of any age.
