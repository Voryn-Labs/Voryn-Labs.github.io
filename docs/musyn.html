Musyn

Music, in your rhythm.

A high-fidelity iPhone music player uniting YouTube Music and JioSaavn into one serene, ad-free listening experience. Featuring dynamic time-synced karaoke lyrics, Dynamic Island playback indicators, offline audio caching, and zero surveillance telemetry.

Interface Gallery

Captured directly from iPhone. High-contrast typography, live lyrics synchronization, and distraction-free audio controls.

Engineered for Listening Freedom

Software designed to get out of the way and keep the music front and center.

Dual-Source Audio Streaming

Search and stream seamlessly across YouTube Music and JioSaavn (up to 320 kbps AAC) from one unified search bar without requiring account credentials.

Deterministic Offline Storage

Download tracks and entire albums directly to your iPhone filesystem for uninterrupted playback when traveling or offline.

Zero Telemetry & Algorithm Hijacking

No recommendation engines forcing promoted tracks into your queue. No behavioral profiling, no tracking pixels, and zero advertisements.

CarPlay & Home Screen Widgets

Includes CarPlay support (when signed with compatible entitlements) and native iOS Home Screen widgets for immediate resume.

Installation & Sideloading

Distributed as an unsigned IPA. Install via your preferred iOS sideloading utility in seconds.
