Privacy Policy — Musyn

Musyn is an independent, high-fidelity iOS music player uniting YouTube Music and JioSaavn into one unified experience. Built around complete data privacy and local audio storage.

Effective: October 22, 2026 · Bundle: com.vorynlabs.musyn
Direct Endpoints

Audio queries and streams connect directly to official endpoints (YouTube / JioSaavn). Zero intermediary proxy servers.

100% Local Storage

Playlists, listen history, cached album art, and downloaded offline audio are stored strictly in local iPhone memory.

Zero Surveillance SDKs

Musyn has zero advertising libraries, zero telemetry beacons, and zero cross-app tracking identifiers.

1. Requests to Music Streaming Services

Search, catalogue browsing, audio playback, high-resolution artwork, and downloads contact YouTube/YouTube Music directly and, only when configured by the user, JioSaavn. Time-coded lyrics are fetched directly from LRCLIB and configured open lyrics providers.

These requests travel directly between your iPhone and the target API endpoints. Voryn Labs runs no intermediary proxy servers and never intercepts your audio streams.

2. Optional Account Authentication

Connecting your YouTube Music account opens its native WebKit session. Session tokens are securely persisted in your iPhone’s sandboxed local container and are used exclusively to sync your personal library, likes, and playlists. You can disconnect your account anytime in Settings.

3. Device Storage & Offline Caching

Favorite tracks, custom playlists, audio playback history, and downloaded songs reside entirely within your local device sandbox. Removing the application purges all local data. Under LiveContainer or sideloaded environments, credentials remain within the app’s designated sandbox.

4. Redacted Diagnostics

Crash logs and diagnostics never transmit automatically. The Diagnostics screen displays an explicit preview, redacting authentication keys, credentials, and private paths. If you explicitly choose to upload a diagnostic bundle for issue debugging, it is assigned an expiring link that purges automatically.

5. Zero Telemetry Creed

Musyn contains no commercial analytics trackers, no Google Analytics for Firebase, and no advertising software development kits.

6. Direct Studio Correspondence

Questions regarding Musyn’s architectural privacy: appsvorynlabs@gmail.com.

Full source code and auditability: GitHub (Voryn-Labs/Musyn).
