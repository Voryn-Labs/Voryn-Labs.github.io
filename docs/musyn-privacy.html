Your music and your privacy.

Updated 19 October 2026. This describes Musyn, the iPhone music player by Voryn Labs.

Requests to music services

Search, catalogue browsing, playback, artwork and downloads contact YouTube/YouTube Music and JioSaavn. Lyrics may be requested from LRCLIB. Those services receive requests needed for the feature, including your IP address and search or track information, and apply their own policies. Availability is not guaranteed.

Optional account connection

Connecting YouTube Music opens its sign-in flow. Session cookies are stored in this installation’s local app storage and used for account features such as playlists and likes. They are sensitive credentials; do not include them in bug reports. Disconnect the account in Settings when you no longer want it linked.

Saved on your device

Favorites, history, preferences, playlists, cached metadata and downloaded audio are stored locally. Removing the app or its container removes that local data under the installer’s and iOS’s storage rules. A LiveContainer host controls its guest environment; use a host you trust.

Diagnostics

The diagnostics screen provides app and playback logs for troubleshooting. Sharing is your choice. Review logs before copying them into a public issue, and remove private URLs, account details or credentials.

App analytics and website statistics

Musyn has no advertising or analytics SDK added by Voryn Labs. This website uses the studio’s existing Cloudflare Web Analytics integration for aggregate website statistics. The product page also requests public release data from GitHub to show IPA download totals; this is not an in-app installation tracker. GitHub Pages, GitHub, Cloudflare and Google Fonts receive their own website requests. See the studio website privacy policy.
