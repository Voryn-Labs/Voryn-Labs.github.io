Lab Archive

Twenty-one quiet logic experiments from our founding era.

Before expanding to native desktop accelerators on macOS and sideloaded iOS audio engines, Voryn Labs engineered twenty-one calm, offline puzzle games. Every game was crafted with deterministic rules, zero dark patterns, no forced video ads, and no streak pressure. While active development is paused to focus on our multi-platform software tools, the full collection is cataloged here for posterity.
