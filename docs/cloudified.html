Uncompressed Bit Fidelity

Engineered around preservation of raw image data and reliable recovery.

Original File Preservation

Photos and videos upload without local recompression or loss of EXIF metadata. HEIC, DNG RAW, JPEG, and 4K ProRes MOV files maintain bit-for-bit equality with your local camera roll.

Dual Independent Queues

Google Photos and private Telegram channels operate on separate background workers. Rate-limiting or network timeouts on one destination never block progress on the other.

Atomic Receipt Ledger

Every successfully transferred file is verified against its cryptographic SHA-256 hash and recorded in a local SQLite ledger. Retries are deterministic; zero duplicate uploads.

Large Video Chunk Reconstruction

Large videos exceeding single-file platform limits are automatically split into byte-exact parts accompanied by a reconstruction manifest for flawless reassembly.

Two Complementary Targets

Choose either destination, or back up to both simultaneously for true redundancy.

TARGET A → GOOGLE PHOTOS

Visual Browsing & Search

Full-resolution media sync allowing you to utilize Google Photos search, face detection, and smart album organization while maintaining original asset quality.

TARGET B → TELEGRAM CHANNELS

Private Cold Storage Vault

Uploads originals as uncompressed documents into your private, invite-only Telegram channel. Permanent cloud storage free of recurring monthly subscription pricing.
