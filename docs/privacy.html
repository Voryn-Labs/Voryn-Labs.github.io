Studio Privacy Policy

Voryn Labs is an independent software studio. We engineer considered, privacy-first software across iOS, macOS, Android TV, and handheld devices. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Ever.

1. Studio Architecture & Identity

Voryn Labs operates on first principles: software should serve the user exclusively. This policy explains what information our studio tools, apps, and website collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions can be directed directly to appsvorynlabs@gmail.com.

2. Foundational Era Games & Analytics

For our catalog of founding-era offline puzzle games preserved in the Studio Archive:

  • Anonymous Usage Events: Puzzle completions, level outcomes, settings toggles, and session durations keyed to game IDs, not personal identities. We never collect names, email addresses, or account identifiers.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, and crash stack traces via Firebase Crashlytics used solely for debugging.
  • Purchases: Processed entirely by Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit. We receive entitlement tokens — never payment cards or financial credentials.
  • Local Game Progress: Save states and settings remain exclusively inside your device's local sandbox.

3. Modern Software Tools (Musyn, Cloudified, Excly, Navigy, VividOrbit)

Our modern multi-platform tools operate under zero-telemetry architectures:

  • Excly & Navigy (macOS): Handcrafted in Swift. Zero network calls, zero daemons, zero keystroke logging, and zero external analytics servers.
  • VividOrbit (Android TV): 100% offline channel lineup management. Zero data transmission.
  • Musyn & Cloudified (iOS): Local cryptographic ledgers, direct API communication to chosen user destinations, and zero surveillance telemetry.

4. Your Sovereignty & Data Rights (GDPR, CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, we honor digital sovereignty by default:

  • Deletion & Purge Requests: Write to appsvorynlabs@gmail.com and we will verify and purge any diagnostic records associated with your installation within 30 days.
  • No Data Sale: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal information.
  • Ad Tracking Prevention: Reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via system settings; our software respects device opt-out signals unconditionally.

5. Website Disclosure

The studio website (voryn-labs.github.io) uses native web standards and does not set tracking cookies. Our light/dark theme preference is stored strictly inside your browser's local storage (localStorage) and never transmitted to any server.

6. Direct Correspondence

Voryn Labs — appsvorynlabs@gmail.com. We are committed to transparency, open-source integrity, and user respect.
